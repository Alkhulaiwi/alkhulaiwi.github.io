About

I’m the owner of TekGards in Saudi Arabia and a business partner at Kimmell Cybersecurity in the United States. My work connects technology strategy, cybersecurity, operations, and growth to help organizations modernize with confidence.

I bring business leadership together with deep experience in managed services, cloud and infrastructure architecture, enterprise platforms, networking, security operations, incident response, SIEM, and endpoint security. I also specialize in NIST SP 800-171, CMMC, and DoD cybersecurity requirements, helping organizations build secure, compliant environments that support the mission.

My focus is practical transformation. I use automation and artificial intelligence to improve how organizations operate, scale, and make decisions while ensuring technology and cybersecurity remain aligned with business goals.
